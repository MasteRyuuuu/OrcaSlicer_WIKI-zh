S0 # V2.5.0 Alpha
   P: This is the OrcaSlicer V2.5.0 Alpha, **the most feature-packed release in OrcaSlicer's history**, built from more than 950 pull requests by over 140 contributors. You can now extend the slicer with **Python plugins**, print on **belt printers**, design parametric parts in the experimental **Design tab**, blend new colors from the filaments you have loaded with **color mixing**, give surfaces real 3D texture with **Texture Displacement**, and print two to four parts at once on **IDEX and IQEX printers**. On top of that come the new **Speed Dial**, support for the **Bambu Lab H2C and A2L** with mixed nozzle sizes on the H2D and X2D, much faster startup, slicing and G-code export, Precise Seam modifiers, new infill options, and hundreds of fixes.
   P: **Note:** This is an alpha build for testing. Several of the new features are experimental and may change before the stable release, so back up projects and profiles you care about and please report any problems on GitHub.
S1 # 🧩 Plugins
   P: OrcaSlicer 2.5.0 introduces **Python plugins**, a supported way to add your own features to the slicer without maintaining a fork. A plugin can add one-click scripts, step into slicing to reshape layers or rewrite the G-code, add its own tab or docked panel, or react to events like a finished print. Open **Plugins** from the main menu to install a plugin from a file on your computer or from Orca Cloud; a plugin must ask your permission before it reaches outside OrcaSlicer's data folder, goes online or starts other programs. The plugin system is new and its API may still change, so feedback from users and plugin authors is very welcome. [Getting started with plugins]({{L1}}) · [Plugin development guide]({{L2}})
   P: ☁️ **Find and share plugins on [Orca Cloud]({{L3}}).** Browse the plugins the community has published and subscribe with your Orca Cloud account. OrcaSlicer installs them, offers updates when their authors release new versions, and brings them to every computer you sign in on. Built a plugin of your own? Publish it on Orca Cloud and anyone can use it.

S4 # 🧱 Texture Displacement
   P: **Texture Displacement** stamps a texture onto a model as real, printable relief. When the texture is a colour image, it can also colour the print, mixing filaments to get close to the image's colours.
   P: ☁️ **More textures on [Orca Cloud]({{L71}}).** Beyond the 40-plus built-in textures, you can download texture packs others have shared on Orca Cloud and publish your own there for everyone to use. A texture pack is a plugin, so it installs, updates and publishes the same way.

S5 # 🏭 Belt Printing
   P: This release adds support for belt printers — conveyor ("infinite Z") machines whose gantry is tilted over a moving belt, usually at 45°. Because the belt carries printed material away from the nozzle, a part can be longer than the machine, and a row of parts can print one after another without clearing the bed. Ready-made profiles are included for the Printcepts BabyBelt Pro and the IdeaFormer IR3 V2, plus a Generic Belt Printer you can adapt to other machines such as the CR-30 family. To get started, add one of these printers and slice as usual; the belt options live in Printer settings → Basic information → Belt printer, shown in Advanced mode and above. The [Belt Printing guide]({{L91}}) walks through setup, how slicing for a belt works and what changes in your workflow.

S6 # 👯 iXex Parallel Printing for IDEX & IQEX
   P: OrcaSlicer can now run printers with independent carriages as parallel printers. On an IDEX (two toolheads on one gantry), a dual-gantry machine, or an IQEX (two toolheads on each of two gantries), every active carriage prints its own copy of the part at the same time — either a straight copy or a mirror image — so one print produces two to four parts. OrcaSlicer slices the part once, shows exactly where each carriage will print, keeps parts out of the areas where the carriages could collide, and tells the firmware which mode to run. Ready-made Vivedino Xplorer profiles (IDEX, Dual Gantry and IQEX, plus the single-head model) are included, and any other IDEX or IQEX printer can be set up from its printer settings.

S7 # ✏️ Design Tab (Early Preview)
   P: The Design tab is a new, experimental CAD workspace inside OrcaSlicer. Sketch a profile, constrain and dimension it, turn it into a solid and commit it to the plate — no round trip through another CAD program and an STL. What you build is an editable feature history rather than a mesh, and it is saved inside the 3MF, so you can reopen a project, change a dimension and have the whole part rebuild. The tab is off by default: tick **CAD feature (experimental)** in Preferences → General → Features and restart OrcaSlicer to try it. It is still under active development, so expect changes between builds and keep a backup of projects you care about. [Wiki]({{L130}})

S8 # 🔩 Multi-Nozzle Printers & Extruder Variants
   P: This release adds Bambu Lab's latest multi-nozzle printers and lets the left and right nozzles of the H2D and X2D be different sizes. It also brings **extruder variants** to every printer: one profile can now hold separate settings for a Standard, High Flow or Extra High Flow nozzle, a direct drive or Bowden extruder, and for each nozzle on a multi-nozzle printer. The right values are used automatically for whichever nozzle prints each part.
